OUR IPAD APPS — SETUP
=====================

What's in this folder
  index.html          Start page with links to both apps
  play.html           Play Time: Rory's Hero Academy (9 missions), Brain Games (28 levels), Write,
                      Flashcards, Dot to Dot, I Spy, Draw, Colouring, Silly Faces, Jigsaw, Maze,
                      Feed & Sort, Memory, Animal sounds, Bubbles, Music
  calendar.html       Family Calendar
  *-icon.png          Home-screen icons
  *.appcache          Lets the apps work offline after the first visit

STEP 1 — Put the apps online for free with GitHub Pages (10 minutes, on a computer)
  a. Create a free account at github.com.
  b. Click "+" (top right) > New repository. Name it "ipad", choose Public, click Create repository.
  c. Click "uploading an existing file", drag in ALL the files from this folder, click Commit changes.
  d. Go to Settings > Pages. Under "Branch" choose "main" and "/ (root)", click Save.
  e. Wait a minute or two. Your address will be:  https://YOUR-USERNAME.github.io/ipad/
  Only the app code is online. Your calendar events stay on the iPad itself.

STEP 2 — Add them to the iPad Home Screen
  a. In Safari on the iPad, go to your address from step 1.
  b. Tap Play Time. Tap the Share button (square with an arrow) > Add to Home Screen > Add.
  c. Go back and do the same for Family Calendar.
  Open them from the Home Screen icons from now on. They'll work even with no Wi-Fi.
  Add your calendar events from the Home Screen icon, not from a Safari tab.
  The iPad keeps the two separately, so events entered in Safari won't show up in the icon.

TIPS
  - This iPad is on iOS 12.5.4. Settings > General > Software Update should offer 12.5.7,
    which adds security fixes. Worth installing.
  - No sound? Check the side switch on the iPad isn't set to mute, and turn the volume up.
  - On iPhones (iOS 17 and newer) sounds and the voice now play even with the ring/silent
    switch on. Sound starts after the first tap on the screen; that's an Apple rule.
    If an iPhone is still quiet: turn the volume up with the side buttons while the app is open,
    and check Settings > Accessibility > Spoken Content (for the voice).
    On Android phones, raise the MEDIA volume (not the ringer).
  - Keep kids inside Play Time: Settings > General > Accessibility > Guided Access,
    turn it on, then open Play Time and triple-click the Home button.
  - Calendar: tap a day once to see its events, tap it again to open that day.
    Swipe left/right to move between months, weeks or days.
  - Back up the calendar now and then: Calendars > Back Up Calendar > Email the backup to myself.
  - Rory's Hero Academy: finish missions to earn stars, badges, new capes, hats and worlds.
    Each mission has 5 levels. Finish a mission with at least 3 stars (2 in Memory Match)
    and a big "Level 2 ▶" button appears to go straight to the next, harder level.
    Levels 4 and 5 add things like
    adding and taking away, mixing colours, reading short words, counting sides,
    animal riddles, putting things in order, and feelings and manners.
    The music button (note icon) in the Academy turns the background music on and off.
  - LANGUAGES: press and hold the lock in Play Time and pick English, Français, Español,
    Italiano or Português. The voice, the words on screen and the reading games all change,
    and the app restarts in that language (stars and levels are kept).
    The Family Calendar has its own Language setting under Calendars > Settings.
    The iPad already has a voice for each language. If one sounds robotic, a better one
    can be downloaded free: Settings > General > Accessibility > Speech > Voices.
    Portuguese is written for Portugal (it still works with a Brazilian voice).
  - Brain Games level up by themselves after 4 right answers in a row, and step back
    down if a level is too hard. Press and hold the lock on the Play Time home screen
    to see progress, set Brain Games and Rory levels yourself, change the language,
    and enter your child's name. The grown-ups page scrolls if it's taller than the screen.

WHERE ARE LANGUAGE AND AGE?
  Play Time: on the home screen, PRESS AND HOLD the lock (top-left corner) for about 2 seconds.
    A tap only shows a hint. The "For grown-ups" page opens with Language and Age at the top.
    The version number is at the bottom of that page (v9 or later).
  Family Calendar: tap "Calendars" (top right), then Settings > Language.
  Still the old version? Open the app, wait 10 seconds, close it completely
  (swipe it away in the app switcher), then open it again.

AGES AND WHAT EACH ONE COVERS (Play Time > press and hold the lock > Age)
  Choosing an age changes every learning part of Play Time. Progress is kept separately for each age,
  so you can switch back and forth without losing anything.

  Ages 2-3
    Brain Games: First Words (find the picture, body parts), Colours, Shapes (circle, square,
      triangle and more), Numbers (count to 3, find 1-5, find 1-10), Puzzles (find the same,
      biggest/smallest, shadow match)
    Rory: 3 gentle levels (find the picture, 2 colours, circle or square, count to 3...)
    Write: lines and shapes, name.  Flashcards: colours, shapes, animals, body, feelings, 1-5.
    Smaller I Spy, short Dot to Dots (up to 5 dots), 4-6 piece jigsaws, small mazes.

  Ages 3-4  (what Play Time had before)
    Letters and first sounds, numbers to 20, rhymes, sight words, reading and spelling 3-letter
    words, patterns, puzzles. Rory levels 1-5. Write: shapes, ABC, abc, 123, name.

  Ages 4-5
    Adds: last letter of a word, alphabet order, counting to 20, ABCD patterns,
    first-second-third, telling time to the hour, and a new subject, Our World
    (opposites, weather and clothes, where things come from, day and night, living things).
    Rory moves up: picture-to-word reading, adding to 20, who eats what, ordering 5 things.
    Write adds short words. Flashcards add a Words deck and numbers to 20.
    Bigger jigsaws (up to 16), harder mazes, I Spy with more things.

  Ages 5-6
    Adding and taking away within 20, skip counting by 2s, 5s and 10s, tens and ones,
    comparing numbers to 100, number patterns, time to the half hour, reading and spelling
    longer words, healthy food, float or sink, seasons.
    Rory: missing numbers (4 + ? = 9), remembering 5 colours, equal halves, 3D shapes,
    birds/fish/insects/mammals, sharing equally, measuring, staying safe, solving problems.
    Dot to Dot counts by twos. Flashcards add Sight words. Jigsaws up to 20 pieces.

MUSIC: CELLO
  In Music, the buttons at the top right switch between the xylophone and the new cello.
  Slide a finger along a string like a bow: the faster you move, the louder it plays,
  and holding still keeps the note going softly. The songs work on both instruments.

UPDATING THE APPS LATER (your calendar events and Play Time progress are kept)
  a. On github.com/YOUR-USERNAME/ipad, click "Add file" > "Upload files".
  b. Drag in all the new files (same names) and click Commit changes.
     They replace the old ones. Keep the same repository name, or the address changes.
  c. Wait a minute or two. On the iPad, open each app from its Home Screen icon,
     wait a few seconds, close it, and open it again. The new version appears.
  Tip: back up the calendar first (Calendars > Back Up Calendar) just to be safe.
  Don't delete the Home Screen icons; on some iPads that also deletes their saved data.
